Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026. This policy covers goodcode.ca only.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by The Good Code Company Inc., a federal corporation in Canada.

  • Legal name: The Good Code Company Inc.
  • Federal corporation number: 1833888-4
  • Ontario corporation number: 1001802749
  • Location: Toronto, Ontario, Canada
  • Privacy contact: Privacy Officer, The Good Code Company Inc., hello@goodcode.ca

When this policy says "we", "us", or "our", it means The Good Code Company Inc. When it says "you", it means a visitor to goodcode.ca.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers goodcode.ca only. It does not cover enoch.goodcode.ca, findyourgrants.ca, or any other website, even if we link to them. Those sites need their own privacy policies.

3. What we collect

3.1 Website analytics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics 4, measurement ID G-8DQG18D81S, to understand how visitors use this site, for example which pages are visited and how long a visit lasts. Google Analytics collects, through cookies and similar technologies: pages visited, time of visit, and approximate location (city or region, derived from IP address); device and browser type, screen size, and language setting; how you reached the site, for example a search result or a link. Google Analytics does not collect your name, email address, or phone number from this site. Your IP address is processed by Google to provide the analytics service, and is not used by us to identify you.

Retention: Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months, then deleted automatically.

3.2 Website fonts (Google Fonts)

This site loads fonts (Space Grotesk, Public Sans, and IBM Plex Mono) from Google Fonts. When your browser loads those fonts, Google receives your IP address, browser type, and the page you visited, as it does for any file loaded from a Google server. We do not receive personal information from Google Fonts, and we do not use it to identify you.

3.3 Email enquiries and the contact form

If you email us at hello@goodcode.ca, we receive your email address, your name if you give it, and the contents of your message. If you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address, subject, and message. The contact form is provided by Kit (formerly ConvertKit). When you submit it, your name, email address, subject, and message are sent to Kit, form ID 10249615, and stored in a Kit list operated by us. We use that information only to reply to you and to keep a record of the enquiry. We do not sell email addresses. We do not add you to a mailing list when you email us, unless you separately ask to join one.

Retention: email correspondence is kept for up to 2 years, then deleted, unless a longer retention is needed for a legal or accounting reason, in which case we will say so in our reply.

3.4 What we do not collect

We do not run user accounts, logins, comments, or purchases on this site. We do not collect payment information.

4. Cookies

This site uses cookies in two ways: Google Analytics cookies, which measure visits and pages viewed, set by Google Analytics; and no other cookies set by this site itself. Google Analytics loads only after you accept analytics cookies in the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block Google Analytics cookies, the site will still work. See the Cookie Policy for the full list.

5. Why we collect it, and the lawful basis

  • Analytics: to understand which pages are useful, so we can improve the site. Google Analytics loads only after you accept in the cookie banner. Under GDPR, where it applies, our lawful basis is consent. Under Quebec Law 25, we collect analytics only with your consent.
  • Fonts: to display the site as designed. The fonts are loaded from Google because the site is built that way.
  • Email and contact form: to reply to your enquiry. Under GDPR, where it applies, our lawful basis is legitimate interests in responding to enquiries, and, where you ask us to do something, taking steps before entering into a contract.

6. Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers needed to run this site: Google LLC, for Google Analytics and Google Fonts (Google processes data on servers that may be outside Canada, including in the United States; Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's own privacy policy); our email provider, for mail sent to and from hello@goodcode.ca; and our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, which serves the site files. Kit, for the contact form. Kit processes your name, email address, subject, and message on servers that may be outside Canada. Kit's handling of that data is governed by Kit's own privacy policy. We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with advertisers.

7. Your rights

In Canada (PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25)

You can ask us: what personal information we hold about you; to correct information that is wrong; to delete information we hold about you, subject to legal and accounting retention duties; and how your information has been used and who it has been shared with. Email hello@goodcode.ca with the subject "Privacy request". We will reply within 30 days, as required by PIPEDA, and sooner if we can.

In the European Union and the United Kingdom (GDPR)

If GDPR applies to you, you also have the right to: access, correct, or delete your personal information; restrict or object to processing; receive a copy of your data in a portable form; withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent; and complain to your local data protection authority. Email hello@goodcode.ca with the subject "Privacy request" to exercise any of these rights.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information, including using HTTPS for this site and limiting access to the hello@goodcode.ca mailbox to the company. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

This site is intended for adults and for businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email hello@goodcode.ca and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and publish the new version at https://goodcode.ca/privacy. Material changes will be noted in the policy itself.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a privacy request, go to hello@goodcode.ca, subject "Privacy request".
