01 Company

We build products of our own.

The Good Code Company designs, builds, owns, and operates independent software products. We choose the problems, maintain the systems, and remain accountable for what we ship.

What we do

We develop focused software products and operate them for the long term. We may consult on those products or license customized versions of software we have already built.

What we do not do

We are not an agency. We do not take on client projects, sell development capacity, or build custom software to order.
