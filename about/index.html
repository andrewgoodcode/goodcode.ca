History

The Good Code Company began as an Ontario sole proprietorship on October 15, 2023. The Good Code Company Inc. was incorporated federally and commenced activity in Ontario on October 26, 2026. This page was last updated on November 26, 2026.

What the company does

The company designs, builds, owns, and operates its own systems. It does not take on client projects, sell development capacity, or build custom systems to order. Consulting is available only on systems the company has already built, under a separate written agreement that states scope, deliverables, price, and timeline. The company does not take open-ended briefs.

Products

Enoch. Tamper-evident event logging and documentation archiving for regulated professional firms. In development, pre-launch, pilot discussions underway. See the Enoch page.

Find Your Grants, Canada. Matches Canadian businesses with grants. In development, pre-launch. It is also the first pilot client for Enoch, once it is launched and has transactions. findyourgrants.ca, coming soon.

How the company works

Three principles: systems over projects, scope before scale, and evidence over claims. Correctness, durable architecture, and predictable behaviour matter more than presentation or novelty. One verification example is shown on the home page.
