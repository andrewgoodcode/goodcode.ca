Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026. Analytics cookies load only after you accept them.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored by your browser when you visit a website. This site uses cookies only for analytics, and only with your consent.

Cookies this site uses

  • Google Analytics, _ga: distinguishes visitors. Kept for 2 years. Set only after you accept analytics cookies.
  • Google Analytics, _ga_G-8DQG18D81S: keeps session state for this site. Kept for 2 years. Set only after you accept analytics cookies.
  • Google Analytics, _gid: distinguishes visitors. Kept for 24 hours. Set only after you accept analytics cookies.

Google Fonts, used to display this site, does not set cookies. This site itself sets no other cookies.

Your choice

Google Analytics does not load until you accept analytics cookies in the cookie banner. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and no analytics data is sent. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer of any page. If you change your choice from accepted to declined, we delete the Google Analytics cookies already set (_ga, _ga_8DQG18D81S, _gid) and stop further analytics calls for that visit. You can also clear your browser cookies and visit again, or block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block Google Analytics cookies, the site will still work.

Your consent choice

Your choice is stored in your browser (local storage, key gc-consent) for 12 months, so the banner does not ask again on every visit. Clearing your browser data removes it, and the banner will ask again.
